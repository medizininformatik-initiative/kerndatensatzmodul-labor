## UML Diagram

The class diagram provides an abstract view of the information model and its relationships. ART-DECOR groups are represented as classes connected by associations. A laboratory report is associated with a patient, encounter, healthcare organisation, specimen and laboratory tests. Tests can be categorised into areas and groups, have laboratory parameters and may carry reference ranges. Classes originating in other MII modules use distinct colours.
